# 数据许可询问模板

用途：向 HWD2024、SH17、CIS、SFCHD 等权利人确认本项目的比赛训练、展示和后续使用边界。发送前由项目统筹/指导老师填写真实身份；不得由自动化冒充团队发信。

## 中文模板

主题：关于 `[数据集名称]` 用于高校科技竞赛研究项目的许可咨询

尊敬的 `[作者/数据负责人]`：

您好。我们是 `[学校/学院/团队]`，正在参加由烽火通信科技股份有限公司命题/指导的高校科技竞赛项目“烽眸智鉴”。项目拟研究通信基建施工场景中的人员安全帽/PPE 识别，当前希望评估 `[数据集名称与版本]` 是否可以用于模型研究和比赛演示。

为避免对“公开下载”和许可证范围作错误理解，烦请书面确认以下事项：

1. 是否允许我们在该竞赛中下载、清洗并训练模型？
2. 是否允许在不分发原始数据的前提下，提交/展示模型权重、指标、代码和推理结果？
3. 是否允许在答辩 PPT、网页、技术方案和演示视频中展示少量标注帧？如需打码或限制数量，请告知。
4. 是否允许发布数据统计、类别映射或衍生标注？如允许，适用什么许可证？
5. 若比赛由企业命题、可能有奖金、成果转让或后续企业试用，是否仍属于当前许可范围？哪些情形需要另行签署商业授权？
6. 数据中可识别人物、第三方图片、拍摄地点或上游素材是否还有额外的肖像、隐私、署名或展示限制？
7. 请确认我们应使用的正式引用、版本号、版权声明和许可证链接。

我们的预期做法是：原始数据只存放在受控环境，不放入公开代码仓库；公开材料尽量使用脱敏样例；保存下载日期、LICENSE 和原始制品 SHA-256；正式现场指标另用取得授权的通信施工视频，不把公共数据分数表述为现场泛化结果。

如上述用途可以被许可，烦请明确回复允许/不允许的项目及任何附加条件。感谢您的时间。

联系人：`[姓名、学校邮箱]`  
项目/竞赛说明：`[官方链接或附件]`  
计划使用的数据版本：`[版本、下载页]`

## English template

Subject: Permission inquiry for using `[dataset name/version]` in a university technology competition

Dear `[dataset author or rights holder]`,

We are a student team from `[university/department]` participating in a university technology competition with a problem proposed or advised by FiberHome Telecommunication Technologies Co., Ltd. Our project studies PPE and safety-helmet recognition in telecommunications construction scenarios.

To avoid treating public availability as permission, could you please confirm in writing whether we may:

1. download, clean, and use `[dataset]` to train models for this competition;
2. submit or demonstrate model weights, metrics, code, and inference results without redistributing the original dataset;
3. show a small number of annotated frames in slides, a web demo, a technical report, and a demo video;
4. release aggregate statistics, class mappings, or derivative annotations, and under which license;
5. use the results when the competition is company-sponsored, may include prizes or technology transfer, or may lead to a later company trial;
6. process images containing identifiable people or third-party material, including any additional privacy, publicity, attribution, or display restrictions;
7. use the official citation, version, copyright notice, and license link you specify.

We plan to keep the original data in controlled storage, exclude it from public source repositories, de-identify public examples where necessary, retain the LICENSE/download date/SHA-256, and use separately authorized telecommunications-site video for any final field evaluation.

Please indicate which uses are permitted, prohibited, or require additional terms. Thank you.

Contact: `[name and institutional email]`  
Competition information: `[official link or attachment]`  
Dataset version and page: `[version and URL]`
